**Project 1: Retail Business Performance**

This project focused on analyzing retail business performance to identify sales trends, customer behavior patterns, and areas for operational improvement. Using Excel, I cleaned and organized raw sales data, performed exploratory analysis, and created summary reports to track key performance metrics. The analysis helped uncover underperforming product categories and seasonal trends, leading to actionable insights that could improve revenue growth and decision-making.
